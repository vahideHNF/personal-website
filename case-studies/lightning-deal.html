Digikala · Seller advertising · 2022–2024

Why weren't sellers buying the most-viewed ad slot?

Lightning Deal put a seller's discount on Digikala's most-viewed page. It earned a quarter of seller-panel revenue, yet most sellers never used it. The fix was asking for one decision per step.

My role
UX Researcher
Team
Product, design and UX writing
Company
Digikala, Iran's largest e-commerce marketplace
Methods
Analytics, interviews, survey, usability tests

In short

  • Problem
    Lightning Deal brought in 25% of seller-panel revenue, but only 38% of sellers used it and 42% of those who started finished.
  • Insight
    Sellers didn't know it existed, couldn't tell how it worked, or didn't know what would pay off. The flow asked for too many decisions at once.
  • Impact
    A one-decision-per-step flow with personalised hints: +17% campaigns, funnel success 42% → 74%.
