Snapp! · Periodic driver survey · 2025–2026

What do drivers need to know before they say yes to a long trip?

I surveyed long-distance drivers, then asked again nine months later. Round 1 reshaped the ride card and bidding. Round 2 showed that every fix had moved the bottleneck somewhere new.

My role
UX Researcher (lead)
Team
Product managers and designers
Timeline
Autumn 2025 and summer 2026
Methods
Repeated survey, persona breakdowns, A/B tests

Numbers on this page are illustrative. Study data is confidential under NDA, so these figures are changed. The findings, the direction of every change and the decisions are real. The real measured results of the shipped changes are listed in my resume.

In short

  • Problem
    No measured picture of why drivers took, skipped or cancelled long-distance rides, and no way to tell if things improved.
  • Insight
    Half of drivers couldn't decide from the ride card. Once that was fixed, competition and passenger cancellations took its place.
  • Impact
    Passenger and luggage details on the ride card: 11× luggage-preference use, +1.9% acceptance. A verified-passenger badge added +1.6%.
