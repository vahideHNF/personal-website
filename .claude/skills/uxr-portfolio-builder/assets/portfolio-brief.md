## 5. Case studies (choose 2 to 4)

Pick the projects you'd most like to talk about in an interview. Copy this block for each one. **Attach the report, deck or one-pager if you have it** (remove anything secret first): Claude pulls most answers from it.

```
### Project: _______________________

Company / product area:            e.g. FoodNow · Checkout
When, and how long:                e.g. spring 2025, 6 weeks
My role:                           e.g. lead researcher; worked with 2 PMs, 1 designer, 1 analyst
Methods and sample sizes:          e.g. 14 interviews, survey with 1,200 responses, 2 rounds of usability tests (10 people)

The problem (with a number if possible):
  e.g. 38% of carts were abandoned at the payment step.

What the team believed before the research:
  e.g. "Users leave because of the delivery fee."

What you found (key findings, with numbers):
  e.g. Fee mattered less than surprise: 7 of 14 interviewees left when the total jumped at the last step.

The surprise or the "aha":
  e.g. Fee level barely mattered; seeing it late did.

What changed because of the research (shipped, tested, decided, dropped):
  e.g. Fees shown on the menu page (shipped); free-delivery threshold (tested, dropped).

Measured result after launch (if any):
  e.g. checkout completion +9%, support tickets about fees −30%

A participant quote you can share (optional):
  e.g. "I don't mind paying, I mind being tricked." — regular customer

Visuals you can share (optional): cleaned screenshots, before/after designs
```
