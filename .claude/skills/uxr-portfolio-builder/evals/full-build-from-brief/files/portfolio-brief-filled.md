## 5. Case studies

### Project: Early churn at Fernwise
- When: autumn 2023, 8 weeks. Role: lead researcher, with 1 PM, 2 designers, 1 data analyst.
- Methods: analytics, 3-week diary study with 12 new users, survey with 1,800 responses.
- Problem: 58% of new users stopped opening the app within 2 weeks.
- Team believed: too many budget categories; bank sync errors; no reminders.
- Found: users who went over budget in week 1 were 2.6x more likely to quit. Diary entries were full of guilt: the red "Over budget!" banner made people avoid the app. Category count and sync made little difference (sync errors only hit 6% of churned users).
- Surprise: it was shame, not friction.
- Shipped: replaced red alerts with a calm "adjust your plan" prompt; weekly "small wins" summary.
- Result: week-4 retention +9.4%, budget edits +27%.
- Quote: "I didn't want to open it and get told off again."
- Tested and dropped: fewer categories (no effect).

### Project: Segmentation for auto-save at Fernwise
- When: spring 2024, 6 weeks. Role: lead, with data science.
- Survey 2,400 responses + behavioural data, latent class analysis -> 4 segments: Planners (31%), Strugglers (27%), Hands-off (24%), Avoiders (18%).
- Auto-save was pitched to everyone; Planners and Hands-off wanted it (64% and 58% interested), Strugglers feared overdrafts (only 19% interested), Avoiders didn't engage.
- Shipped: auto-save offer targeted to Planners and Hands-off, with an overdraft guard. Opt-in +15.2%.
- Next: a "safety buffer" feature for Strugglers (in development).

### Project: Trust in video appointments at Tilia Health
- When: 2021-2022. Role: researcher, with PM, designer.
- 20 interviews (12 patients, 8 doctors), then 2 rounds of usability tests (8 patients each) on the waiting-room redesign.
- Problem: 1 in 5 video appointments ended in a no-show; patients said they "didn't know if the doctor was real or when they'd join".
- Found: trust is built before the call: seeing the doctor's photo and credentials, and knowing the wait time. 9 of 12 patients mentioned not knowing how long they'd wait.
- Shipped: waiting room with doctor profile, live queue position, and a test-your-camera step.
- Result (public, Tilia blog 2023): no-shows dropped from 21% to 13%. Round 2 usability: 8 of 8 completed the camera check vs 5 of 8 in round 1.
- Quote: "When I saw her face and that I was second in line, I relaxed."
